## Full description (≤ 4000 chars)
Tirelo Services connects you with verified local service providers across
Botswana — plumbers, electricians, mechanics, cleaners, carpenters and many
more. Browse by category, compare ratings and prices, pick a provider near you,
choose a time and location on the map, and pay securely with Orange Money,
Mascom MyZaka, card or bank transfer.

Features:
• Discover & search verified providers, sorted by distance, rating or price
• Real-time chat with your provider (typing indicators, read receipts, images)
• Secure in-app payments and instant booking confirmation
• Ratings & reviews from real, completed bookings
• Saved addresses, favourites and recently viewed providers
• Push notifications for every step of your booking
• Dark mode and full offline awareness

For providers: manage your profile and portfolio, set availability, accept
bookings, chat with customers and track your earnings with analytics.
