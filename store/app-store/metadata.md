## Description (≤ 4000 chars)
Tirelo Services is the easiest way to find and book trusted local service
providers across Botswana. Browse by category, compare ratings and prices,
choose a provider near you, pick a date, time and map location, and pay securely
with Orange Money, Mascom MyZaka, card or bank transfer.

• Discover & search verified providers by distance, rating or price
• Real-time chat (typing indicators, read receipts, image sharing)
• Secure payments and instant booking confirmation
• Ratings & reviews from completed bookings
• Saved addresses, favourites, recently viewed
• Push notifications, dark mode, offline awareness

Providers can manage profiles and portfolios, set availability, accept bookings,
chat with customers and track earnings.
