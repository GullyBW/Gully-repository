# Motse — Investor & Partner One-Pager

**Digital Public Infrastructure for Botswana — a trusted rail for civic, cultural, and financial life.**

---

### The problem
Digital services in Botswana are **fragmented**: every app, bank, campaign, and
ministry re-builds its own identity, payments, and records — duplicating cost,
fragmenting the citizen experience, and leaving feature-phone and rural users
behind. Trust is *asserted*, not *proven*: citizens cannot easily verify that
funds moved or that a community campaign spent honestly.

### The solution
**Motse** is a shared, sovereign platform where every service inherits three
public primitives — a **verified identity graph**, a **double-entry ledger**, and
a **tamper-evident governance/audit engine** — making each service *trustworthy by
construction*. On top sit ten civic, cultural, and financial services, inclusive
payments (mobile money, cards, diaspora remittance), and full **USSD/SMS + offline
reach**.

### Why now
A national **Vision 2036** digital agenda, mature mobile-money rails, high mobile
penetration, and a global **Digital Public Infrastructure** wave (proven by
India's identity/payments stack) make this the moment for a Botswana-owned DPI.

### The differentiator — a platform that proves itself
Motse is **self-governing**: it reconciles its own reporting against the
authoritative ledger, measures the effectiveness of its own decisions, briefs
leadership in one evidence-backed view, learns from outcomes, and **fails its own
build if evidence quality regresses**. Every metric has a source; every
recommendation has evidence. For a system holding public money and public trust,
this is the moat.

### Traction — a working reference implementation
Not a slide deck: a production-shaped build already exists — the three primitives,
ten services, three payment rails, and the full enterprise-operations and
intelligence layer — validated by an **extensive automated test suite, ≥95%
coverage gates on new code, and a fault-injection harness** that proves the
telemetry tells the truth under failure.
