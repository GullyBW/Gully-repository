# Motse — Letter of Proposal

**To:** The Permanent Secretary, [Ministry of Communications, Knowledge & Technology]
**From:** [Motse Platform Initiative]
**Date:** [Date]
**Re:** Proposal to establish Motse — a national Digital Public Infrastructure platform

---

Dear Permanent Secretary,

On behalf of the Motse Platform Initiative, I am writing to propose the
establishment of **Motse** — a sovereign **Digital Public Infrastructure (DPI)**
platform for Botswana — and to request the Ministry's endorsement of a
community-scale pilot.

Botswana's Vision 2036 commits the nation to becoming a diversified,
knowledge-based, and digitally-enabled society. Delivering on that ambition
requires more than another portfolio of apps; it requires a **shared, trusted
digital foundation** on which government, communities, and the private sector can
all build. Today, identity, payments, and record-keeping are re-implemented
service by service — duplicating cost, fragmenting the citizen experience, and
leaving those on feature phones and in rural areas behind.

Motse proposes a different foundation. Every service is built on **three shared
public primitives**: a **verified identity graph**, a **double-entry money-
movement ledger**, and a **tamper-evident governance and audit engine**. Because
these are shared, every service on Motse is *trustworthy by construction* — money
always balances, every action is auditable with public proofs, and access is
always governed. Critically for a public system, Motse is **self-validating**: it
continuously reconciles its own reporting against the authoritative financial
ledger and can produce signed, machine-verifiable evidence that it is operating
correctly.

The platform is deliberately **inclusive** — the same services work over USSD and
SMS as in the app, with offline data packs for low-connectivity areas — and
deliberately **respectful of culture**, with a consent-first heritage archive that
protects communities' ownership of their oral history and language.

We do not ask the Ministry to endorse an idea alone. A **working reference
implementation already exists** — the shared primitives, ten citizen and
institutional services, mobile-money and card payment rails, and a complete
enterprise-operations layer — demonstrated by an extensive automated test suite
and a fault-injection validation harness. This proves the architecture is
feasible, auditable, and production-shaped.

We respectfully request:

1. The Ministry's **endorsement in principle** of Motse as a national DPI
   initiative aligned to Vision 2036 and the national digital agenda;
2. Nomination of a **sponsoring team** to co-design a limited, community-scale
   **pilot** (for example, a district kgotla governance and community-fundraising
   deployment); and
3. A **working session** to align on data sovereignty, residency, and regulatory
   requirements.

The accompanying project proposal sets out the opportunity, architecture,
beneficiaries, phased roadmap, governance and security posture, risks, and
success metrics in full. We would welcome the opportunity to present it to the
Ministry and its partners.

Motse is offered in the spirit of its name — a digital *home* for the civic,
cultural, and financial life of Botswana, owned by and accountable to its people.
