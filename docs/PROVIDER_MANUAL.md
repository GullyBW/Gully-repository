# Provider manual

## Set up your business
1. Register choosing *Service provider*, verify your email.
2. Open **Provider dashboard** → complete your profile: business name, primary
   category, bio, years of experience, starting price, languages and base
   location. Upload a profile photo and **portfolio** images.
3. An admin reviews and grants the **Verified** badge — verified providers rank
   higher and earn customer trust.

## Manage availability
- In the dashboard **Availability** tab set working days, hours and slot length.
- Toggle **emergency availability**, or **vacation mode** with an end date.
- Set your live status (available / busy / offline / vacation). Customers only
  see open slots.

## Handle bookings
1. You receive a **New booking** notification (and in **Bookings**).
2. **Accept** or **decline**. After accepting: **Start work** → **Complete**.
3. **Message** the customer for coordination; share images/location.
4. The customer pays in-app; you get a **Payment received** notification.

## Track performance
- **Earnings & analytics**: monthly earnings, bookings, acceptance rate, repeat
  customers, ratings. **Export** to CSV / Excel / PDF.

## Tips
- Respond quickly (response time is shown to customers and used in ranking).
- Keep a strong rating — only customers with completed bookings can review you.
