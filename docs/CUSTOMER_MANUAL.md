# Customer manual

## Getting started
1. **Register** with your name, email, phone and password (choose *Customer*).
2. **Verify your email** via the link sent to you.
3. Optionally **enable push notifications** in Settings.

## Finding & booking a provider
1. On **Home**, pick a service category or use the search bar.
2. Browse results — filter by *Available* / *Verified*, sort by nearest, rating,
   price, jobs or response time, and tap **Near me** to rank by distance.
3. Open a provider to see their profile, ratings and reviews. Tap the heart to
   **favourite** them.
4. Tap **Book now** and complete the wizard: date → time slot → location (search,
   drop a pin, or use a saved address) → details → summary.
5. After the provider **accepts**, tap **Pay** and choose Orange Money, MyZaka,
   card or bank transfer. Follow the prompt; the booking confirms once payment
   settles.

## During & after the job
- **Message** your provider from the booking screen (images, typing indicators,
  read receipts). React to, forward, copy or delete messages; report or block if
  needed.
- When complete, **leave a review** (1–5 stars + comment).

## Your account
- **Recently viewed**, **Favourites**, **Saved addresses**, **Payment history**,
  **My reviews** and **My activity** (spending/usage charts) are in Profile.
- **Settings**: dark mode, notification categories, active sessions (revoke a
  device), log out.
