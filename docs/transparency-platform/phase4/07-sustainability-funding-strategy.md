# Phase 4 · 07 — Sustainability & Funding Strategy

**Traces:** Roadmap `../phase2/06`, Assumptions A-FIN-*, A-OPS-01, RK-15/16/17/18. **Currency:**
BWP primary / USD secondary. **All figures are planning assumptions `⟦validate⟧`, not commitments.**
🔒 Funding approval and procurement are human decisions.

---

## 1. Funding principles (independence-first)

- **The platform cannot be funded by the institutions it scrutinizes** — that would destroy the
  independence its whole trust model rests on (D-01, RK-03). This is the governing constraint.
- **Diversify** to avoid single-funder capture/exit risk (RK-15).
- **Design for low recurring cost** and portability so a funder change does not strand the platform
  (A-FIN-02, technology strategy `06`).

## 2. Implementation funding assumptions `⟦validate⟧`

Sources (to secure): development partners / bilateral & multilateral donors, philanthropic
integrity/anti-corruption funders, and (carefully, without compromising independence) public funds
via an arms-length independent trust. Indicative build+early-ops envelope from `../phase2/06`:
**~BWP 40–90M over Phases 0–2** *(USD ~3–7M)* — **planning-grade, not a quote.**

## 3. Operational funding model

| Cost driver | Notes | FX exposure |
|-------------|-------|-------------|
| Infra & HSM | In-country DC/colo + HSM + offshore DR | Medium (imported HW) |
| Telecom/data | Ops + any zero-rating negotiation (A-FIN-04) 🔒 | Low-Med |
| Specialists | Security/crypto/SOC, some regional/remote (A-OPS-01) | High (USD/EUR) |
| Independent audits | Recurring external assurance (`12`) | High |
| Legal | Ongoing counsel | Medium |
| Governance | Board/committees, transparency reporting | Low |

**Steady-state envelope (Phase 6):** **~BWP 20–40M/yr** *(USD ~1.5–3.1M)* `⟦validate⟧`. FX
contingency built in (A-FIN-03).

## 4. Staffing & skills strategy

- **Blend:** in-country core team + regional/remote specialists under operator-in-threat-model
  controls (JIT, dual control, audit).
- **Skills development:** deliberate local-capacity pipeline (training, mentoring, knowledge
  transfer) to reduce long-run dependence on foreign specialists (RK-18) — a sustainability
  investment, not just a cost.
- **Key-person risk:** documented runbooks, cross-training, succession (esp. governance custodians).

## 5. Vendor management & procurement 🔒

- **Avoid lock-in** (portable, open-standards architecture); prefer terms allowing exit/portability.
- **Managed services** (SOC, audit, hosting) held to operator-in-threat-model controls + SOC 2
  where relevant (`03`).
- **Procurement is a human governance decision** (transparent, conflict-of-interest-controlled) —
  never automated; independent-auditor and HSM procurement flagged 🔒.

## 6. Long-term sustainability plan

- **Endowment / multi-year commitments** to smooth funding volatility.
- **Low idle-cost architecture** (scales down when quiet) to reduce burn.
- **Value evidence** via the Benefits Realization Framework (`08`) + Public Trust Index to sustain
  funder and public support.
- **Sustainability review** by the Oversight Board with published financials (transparency).

## 7. Honesty on funding risk

⚠️ The single biggest sustainability threat is **funding tied to independence**: the cheapest money
often comes with strings that compromise the platform's neutrality. The strategy explicitly trades
*some* funding ease for *durable independence*, and names the risk (RK-15/17) rather than hiding it.
If independent funding cannot be secured, that is a **go/no-go finding for decision-makers**, not a
detail.
