## Statement-type convention (required by the brief)

Every material statement is one of:
- **[FACT]** — verifiable from an approved NJTIP artifact or widely-documented public fact.
- **[ASSUMPTION]** / `⟦validate⟧` — Botswana-specific or forward-looking; requires validation.
- **[DECISION]** — a ratified decision (D-01…D-11) or a governance decision recorded here.
- **[REC]** — a recommendation for the programme to adopt.
- **🔒 [HUMAN-REVIEW]** — reserved for qualified human experts (constitutional, legislation,
  judicial procedure, admissibility, institutional governance, procurement, funding, cross-border,
  key custody, national policy). AI supports, never replaces, these.

## Where this leaves the programme

Phase 5 is the **execution scaffolding**. It does **not** authorize go-live — the Operational
Readiness Gate (`../phase2/05`), Production Go-Live Gate (`../phase3/08`), the Production Evidence
Package (`../phase4/13` + `06`), and Oversight Board sign-off do. Build/validation on **synthetic
data** proceeds; **no production data flows** until gates are GREEN and the 🔒 human reviews land.
